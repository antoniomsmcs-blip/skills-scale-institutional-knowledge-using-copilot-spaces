# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central guide to OctoAcme's project management processes and the documents that support them.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate projects by confirming the business need, stakeholders, success measures, and authorization to proceed. They then plan shippable increments, prioritize and estimate the backlog, define acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, and identify milestones, risks, and dependencies. During execution, teams coordinate through standups, delivery syncs, demos, project boards, reviews, and testing while tracking progress and escalating blockers. Before release, they verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans; after deployment, they verify and communicate outcomes. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents turn lessons into owned, trackable improvements. Clear roles, regular stakeholder communication, and evidence-based decisions support the lifecycle.
